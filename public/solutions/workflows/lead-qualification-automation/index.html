Human boundaries

Automate the repetition. Keep the judgment.

Runs on its own

  • Enrichment, criteria matching, and tiered classification
  • Written explanations for every classification
  • CRM updates and routing to owners
  • Standard follow-up triggers per tier

Stops for a human

  • Final pursuit decisions and pricing conversations
  • Borderline fits where strategy matters more than rules
  • Anything resembling an automated yes/no on a real relationship

AI should explain, not decide your pipeline. Classification comes with reasons; irreversible choices — pursuing, pricing, discarding — stay with you.
