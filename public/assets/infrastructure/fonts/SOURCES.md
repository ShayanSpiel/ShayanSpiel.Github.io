# Public infrastructure type assets

The two static Outfit TTFs are derived from the public website's
`public/assets/fonts/outfit-latin.woff2`, at weights 500 and 800.
Variable axes were instantiated and overlapping contours were merged before
export. Merging is necessary for correct signed-distance-field rendering.
Outfit is licensed under the SIL Open Font License.

DM Serif Display uses the website's existing
`public/assets/fonts/dm-serif-display-latin.ttf`. It is the title accent only.
No font or logo is fetched from the private company home.
