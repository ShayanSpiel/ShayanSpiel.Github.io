BEFORE YOU PAY

Working together: the details

Why monthly rather than hourly?

Hours are not the result. Agree one problem and acceptance criteria; the monthly rate includes delivery, tests and fixes within that scope.

What does one active build mean?

Work on one agreed problem. Start the next after completion; the commitment does not cover several parallel projects.

What counts as a build?

A bounded problem with a reviewable result, such as a workflow with defined sources, tools, ownership and acceptance. Define its boundaries in the initial review.

What if it takes longer than a month?

The subscription covers the active build while work continues. Expected timing and continuation terms are defined in the proposal before starting.

What if it misses acceptance criteria?

The build is unfinished. Fixes needed to meet the agreed result belong to that same scope.

Are revisions included?

Yes, corrections needed for the agreed result. A new outcome or materially changed requirement needs a new scope.

Is a long contract or a call required?

No. Start by sending an example of the work. After handoff, define the next problem or stop.
