# Playbook Coherence

Quick rules for keeping a Worker's playbook consistent.

## Rules

1. **One source of truth per playbook.** If you split it, justify in
   the playbook header.
2. **Workflow steps, not instructions.** Each step should be a verb
   phrase ("Research", "Prepare outreach") not a paragraph.
3. **Reference skills inline.** "Research → skill: company-research"
   so the playbook stays under one screen.
4. **No metric auto-promotion.** A playbook step that tracks a metric
   is workflow, not a Goal.
5. **No strategy embedded.** "Use the approved ICP" is a reference;
   "we focus on accountants" is Strategy and belongs elsewhere.
