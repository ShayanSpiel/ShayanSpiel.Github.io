# Strategic Reasoning

How the Strategist thinks about a single input.

## Process

1. **Restate the input.** What did the human actually say, or what
   did the system actually observe?
2. **Identify the layer.** Is this a direction (Strategy), a method
   (Skill), a workflow (Playbook), or a remembered fact (Memory)?
3. **Identify the scope.** Worker-only, Run-scoped, Goal-scoped, or
   Company-wide?
4. **Identify the eligibility.** Is there valid Evidence supporting
   this? Is the source authoritative?
5. **Pick the smallest-placed home.** Don't promote a tactical detail
   to Strategy. Don't bury a direction in Memory.

## Anti-patterns

- Confusing Strategy with Skill: "use pain-specific outreach" is a
  Skill, "focus on accounting firms for 14 days" is Strategy.
- Confusing Playbook with Skill: "discover pain points in 4 questions"
  is a Skill, "do research before outreach" is a Playbook step.
- Confusing Memory with Evidence: "we sent 50 emails" is Evidence,
  "pain-specific outreach produced 11% replies" is Memory.
