faq:
  - q: "What does it mean to automate recruitment with AI?"
    a: "It means running the coordination loop between brief and interview: capture the vacancy brief, source candidates, screen CVs, score matches, rank a shortlist, schedule interviews, and hand over the ranked result. It does not mean a chatbot answering candidates or a model deciding who gets hired. The recruiter reviews the output and makes the calls that matter."
  - q: "How does AI candidate screening work?"
    a: "The vacancy brief is structured into must-have criteria: required stack, years, location, work preference, and salary band. Each CV is read against those criteria, scored, and given fit reasons and gaps. The result is consistent, explainable screening instead of a recruiter judging every résumé from memory of what a strong candidate looks like."
  - q: "How is CV screening different from candidate matching?"
    a: "CV screening scores each résumé against the brief. Candidate matching pairs the brief criteria with the candidate pool from a database, LinkedIn, or Indeed and ranks the strongest fits. Screening evaluates, matching compares and orders. Both run on the same structured criteria and both show their work."
  - q: "Do I need a new ATS or platform to automate recruitment?"
    a: "No. The loop runs on top of LinkedIn, Indeed, your email, calendar, and Drive. It adds the coordination layer while your existing stack stays exactly where it is. You do not migrate platforms and you do not hand hiring decisions to AI."
  - q: "What parts of recruitment should stay human?"
    a: "Judgment calls: fit beyond the checklist, sensitivity in conversations, the actual interview, and the offer. Automation handles the tracking, chasing, and filing so the recruiter spends the day recruiting instead of coordinating."
  - q: "Where does AI recruitment automation break?"
    a: "A vague brief produces vague matching, automation inherits whatever criteria you give it, and it cannot fix a weak candidate pool. Define must-haves before automating, review the criteria for bias the way you review a job ad, and keep sourcing fresh or the ranking will rank a weak pool well."
  - q: "How do I start automating recruitment with AI?"
    a: "Pick one coordination workflow, not the whole function: role intake, CV screening, shortlist prep, or interview scheduling. Get it reviewed against your real stack, agree the scope and acceptance criteria before any build, and keep the ranked shortlist in front of a recruiter."
