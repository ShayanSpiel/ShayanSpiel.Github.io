<SectionHead tag='The temptation' title='Everyone Wants a Marketing Taxonomy' sub='Feature hubs, solution pages, glossaries, directories. If you use AI to generate your site structure, the temptation is a parallel fiction — a structure that describes a product you did not actually build. Search engines are just another user who will notice.' />

<ProseCenter text='The SpielOS site exists to send qualified visitors to the waitlist. The shortest honest path is to describe what the product actually is. SpielOS is a harness: roles, skills, workflows, evals, context, providers, connections. That is the architecture, and it is also the sitemap.' />

<ProseCenter text='The instruction I gave myself was blunt: do not invent a separate marketing taxonomy. No parallel directory, no solution pages, no teams or templates that do not exist in the product. The features the product ships are the features the site ranks for.' />

<Takeaway text='A marketing taxonomy that does not match the product is content debt you will pay forever.' />

<section class='section'>
  <SectionHead tag='Why it works' title='Search Wants What the Product Is' sub='When model capability converges, the differentiator is the harness around it. Search engines are just another user of that same answer.' />

  <ProseCenter text='The search result for "agent harness" should match a page about the harness because that is what the product is. The search result for "workflow execution" should match the workflows page because workflows are how execution happens. No translation layer, no marketing gloss.' />

  <Pullquote quote='The fastest SEO is honesty about what you built. The architecture is the answer; everything else is decoration.' />

  <ProseCenter text='This is the same decision that should shape how you use AI to present your own business: write about what the system actually does, including the parts that broke. Product truth compounds. Marketing fiction does not.' />
</section>

<section class='section'>
  <SectionHead tag='Apply it' title='Architecture-First SEO' sub='Three moves that cost almost nothing and change everything — especially if you generate pages with AI.' />

      <div class='step-num'>1</div>
      <div class='step-body'>
        <strong>Map the real product.</strong>

      <div class='step-num'>2</div>
      <div class='step-body'>

    </div>
    <div class='step-item'>
      <div class='step-num'>3</div>
      <div class='step-body'>
        <strong>Mirror it in every language.</strong>
        One architecture, mirrored wrappers. Bilingual SEO becomes a routing problem, not a content problem.
      </div>
    </div>

  <Takeaway text='Stop optimizing the homepage. Optimize the truth. The architecture already did the keyword research.' />
