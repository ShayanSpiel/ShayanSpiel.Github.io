{% include components/section-head.html tag='Approach' title='How marketing strategy connects to agent architecture' sub='The same structure applies to both.' %}
Marketing

Workflow design

Every campaign was a sequence of steps, decision points, handoffs, and measurements. The same decomposition applies to agent workflows.

Marketing

Measurement

If you cannot measure a campaign, you cannot improve it. The same principle applies to agent evals and trajectory analysis.

Marketing

Decision systems

Budget allocation, audience targeting, and channel selection are decision systems. Agent authority and tool selection follow the same logic.

Marketing

Customer behavior

Understanding what users actually do versus what they say they do transfers directly to understanding how agents behave versus how we expect them to.

Marketing

Operational constraints

Budget limits, timeline pressure, and team size force clarity. Agent systems face the same constraints with tokens, latency, and model capability.

Marketing

Structured execution

A campaign without a brief is improv. An agent without a specification is the same thing.
