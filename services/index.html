{% include components/section-head.html tag='Process' title='How each service works' sub='Every engagement follows the same five-step structure, adapted to the service.' %}
Step 1

Evidence

I review workflow examples, traces, prompts, tool definitions, architecture documents, and team input to understand the current or intended system.

Step 2

Workflow

The operational workflow is mapped end to end. Decision points, handoffs, exceptions, and human loops are documented before any agent design begins.

Step 3

System

The agent architecture is defined or audited: roles, tools, context, memory, state, orchestration, approval points, and integration boundaries.

Step 4

Evals

Acceptance criteria, representative scenarios, rubrics, trajectory checks, and regression gates are designed from real workflow evidence.

Step 5

Decision

A clear recommendation: build, revise, buy, refactor, or stop, with evidence supporting the choice.
