{% include components/section-head.html tag="Process" title="Five steps from problem to decision" sub="A structured engagement that ends with a clear recommendation." %}
Step 1

Evidence

Gather traces, logs, prompts, context dumps, tool definitions, and current architecture docs. Interview the team about pain points and goals.

Step 2

Workflow

Map the current or intended workflow. Identify decision points, authority boundaries, human loops, and failure modes.

Step 3

System

Design or audit the agent system architecture: roles, tools, context, memory, orchestration, and integration points.

Step 4

Evals

Define acceptance criteria, evaluation traces, scoring, regression tests, and human approval gates.

Step 5

Decision

A final recommendation: Build, Revise, Buy, Refactor, or Stop — with supporting evidence for each.
