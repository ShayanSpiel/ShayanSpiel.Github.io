# Persian copywriting

Use the same workflow and authorities as `copywriting-en`:

1. Read `.agents/company/strategy/icp.md`, `positioning.md`, and `voice.md`.
2. Simulate the reader's real situation before writing.
3. Draft one idea or story, then make the platform edit.

This skill is a language adapter, not a second strategy. Do not redefine the ICP, offer, voice, proof, or platform rules.

## Persian quality

- Write original, natural Persian. Never translate English sentence structure literally.
- Keep the reader, workflow, proof, numbers, names, URLs, IDs, and product facts accurate.
- Use Persian search language and familiar technical terms. Explain a term only when the reader needs it.
- Match readiness and platform context. Make the CTA earned or indirect.
- Keep one idea, short active sentences, practical detail, and supported claims.
- Do not invent evidence or use generic business language.

Return final Persian copy unless notes are requested.
