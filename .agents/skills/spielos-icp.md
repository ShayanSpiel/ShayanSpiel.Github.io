# SpielOS Ideal Customer Profile

Single source of truth for both English and Persian copywriting. Both `copywriting-en/SKILL.md` and `copywriting-fa/SKILL.md` reference this file. Do not create separate English and Persian interpretations of the customer.

---

## Primary customer

SpielOS is primarily for founder-led early-stage startups, solo founders, and small teams that want to operate parts of their company through AI employees and departments.

The primary reader and buyer is usually:

- A technical founder, product-minded founder, or AI-native operator
- Already using models, AI coding tools, agents, prompts, or automations
- Capable of understanding technical ideas but not necessarily an agent-infrastructure specialist
- A systems thinker who wants control, visibility, and repeatability
- Too small to hire a full team for every department
- Unwilling to build a complete agent harness and operating system from scratch
- Skeptical of vague AI claims and generic automation copy

Do not define the ICP only through age, gender, or company size. Its most important characteristics are its situation, technical awareness, pains, desired outcomes, and buying triggers.

---

## Current situation

The customer already has access to capable models but lacks the system around them.

Their prompts, files, tools, automations, and company instructions are scattered. Agents may produce useful answers, but they do not consistently operate like a coordinated team.

Common problems include:

- AI sessions repeatedly starting without sufficient company context
- Important instructions being copied manually between tools
- Company knowledge being scattered across files and services
- Agents answering questions but failing at long-running execution
- No clear model for roles, skills, workflows, memory, or approvals
- Inconsistent output quality
- No reliable evaluation system
- Weak visibility into what agents are doing
- Difficult coordination between multiple agents
- The founder remaining the bottleneck for routine work
- Existing AI employee products feeling like chatbots, wrappers, or rigid automations

---

## Desired outcome

The customer wants to:

- Define AI roles and departments
- Give each role clear instructions
- Assign reusable skills
- Connect files, company knowledge, memory, models, and external tools
- Build repeatable workflows
- Run work through Direct Mode or Director Mode
- Review important decisions
- Approve sensitive actions
- Evaluate output quality
- Preserve useful context across work
- Scale company operations without manually directing every small step

---

## Core product promise

Use this as the canonical positioning idea, not necessarily as identical published copy:

> SpielOS helps founders build and direct AI employees and departments with roles, skills, workflows, context, memory, tools, approvals, and evaluations in one system.

Copy may simplify this based on the surface and awareness level.

Do not dump the entire feature inventory into every headline or paragraph.

## Persian and international markets

The behavioral ICP is largely shared across languages.

### Persian market

Iranian and Persian-speaking founders and builders who are underserved by high-quality Persian and RTL AI products.

Persian copy must:

- Use language Iranian founders naturally understand
- Avoid literal translations of English AI vocabulary
- Explain unfamiliar agent concepts before naming them
- Respect the technical sophistication of the reader without assuming they know internal SpielOS terminology
- Use examples relevant to small startup teams and founder-led operations

### English market

International technical founders, AI-native operators, product builders, and small startup teams.

English copy must:

- Avoid generic SaaS and AI language
- Avoid pretending every reader already understands terms such as harness, orchestration, evals, or persistent context
- Begin with the operational problem or desired outcome
- Introduce technical category language only when it improves understanding
- Sound like a founder explaining a real system, not a venture-funded landing-page generator suffering from adjective poisoning

The Persian market is the initial distribution and localization wedge. It is not a fundamentally different product or customer.

## Awareness levels

Copy must identify which awareness level it addresses.

### Problem-aware

The reader knows their AI workflows are fragmented or unreliable but may not know what an agent harness is.

Lead with:

- Repeated instructions
- Lost context
- Scattered tools
- Inconsistent output
- Founder bottlenecks
- Agents that can chat but cannot reliably execute

### Solution-aware

The reader understands agents, automations, or AI employees but is comparing approaches.

Explain:

- Roles
- Skills
- Workflows
- Memory
- Tool connections
- Approvals
- Evals
- Direct and Director execution

### Product-aware

The reader already knows SpielOS.

Show:

- How the product works
- Specific capabilities
- Product structure
- Real examples
- Tradeoffs
- Setup requirements
- What is currently available

Do not write every page as if it addresses all three levels.

## Buying triggers

Typical triggers include:

- The founder is spending too much time repeating operational work
- The team has accumulated disconnected prompts and automations
- A prototype agent worked, but could not become a reliable workflow
- The company wants AI to handle work across several departments
- Existing tools provide chat or automation but not coordinated execution
- The founder wants to scale output before expanding headcount
- The team needs approvals, traceability, context, and quality control

---

## Primary objections

Copy should address objections when relevant:

- Is this another chatbot or AI wrapper?
- How much setup does it require?
- Can I control what agents are allowed to do?
- Can humans approve important actions?
- Will it work with my models and tools?
- Does it remember company context?
- Can it run real workflows instead of only answering?
- How can I evaluate whether its output is reliable?
- Is it usable by a small team without heavy infrastructure?

Do not answer objections with unsupported superlatives.

---

## Not the primary ICP

SpielOS is not primarily positioned for:

- People seeking a basic chatbot
- Passive users expecting useful AI employees with no configuration or company context
- Large enterprises requiring procurement-heavy deployment
- Developers seeking only a low-level agent framework
- People looking only for a social-content generator
- Buyers expecting autonomous operation with no human oversight

---

## Superseded positioning

Do not use the previous Session-as-Content ICP as the current SpielOS ICP.

The earlier positioning focused on technical founders turning build sessions into publishable content. Work sessions can still provide evidence for founder notes and public content, but content generation is no longer the central SpielOS product promise or primary ICP.
