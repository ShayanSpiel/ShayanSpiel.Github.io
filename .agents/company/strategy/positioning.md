# SpielOS positioning and offer

## Category

SpielOS is the operating system for supervised AI work. It turns a high-volume business workflow into owned operations with roles, context, approvals, evidence, and evaluation.

## Buyer-facing promise

Start with one repetitive workflow that is costing time, capacity, speed, or revenue. Build a supervised system that handles the work, routes exceptions, and shows what happened. Make the owner less necessary without giving up control.

## Commercial paths

- **AI Workers:** hand repetitive team work to supervised AI workflows and agents.
- **AI DeSlopping:** repair bloated or unreliable AI-built software so it stops demanding human babysitting.
- One subscription: $2,990/month, one active build at a time.
- Entry: `/apply/` for **Apply - Free Review**. Scope and acceptance criteria come before payment.
- Booking is optional and secondary. Pricing supports the decision; it does not lead.

## Guardrails

- Lead with the buyer's named workflow and measurable consequence, not feature inventory.
- Claims and proof must be supported. Never promise complete autonomy or instant transformation.
- Use the canonical ICP for targeting. Do not target AI builders, AI agencies, dev shops, or technical harness audiences as buyers.
- A metric can support a true claim, but it cannot replace the promise of removing owner involvement from the daily workflow.
