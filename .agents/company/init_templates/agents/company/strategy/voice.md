# Company voice

- Clear, compact, and specific.
- Bluntly candid. Say the uncomfortable true thing.
- Confident and high conviction. Strong claims are welcome when the evidence earns them.
- Useful and human. Practical and calm.
- Sound like an experienced operator talking to a friend, not a brand announcing a thesis.
- Speak to one senior operator in one real workflow, using their concrete words.
- Name what the person is doing, where the work gets stuck, and what it costs before naming the solution.
- Use natural spoken transitions when useful: "Doing this manually is hard", "Here is where it breaks", "That is the part people miss."
- Avoid generic business language, inflated claims, fake quotes, em dashes, decorative AI symbols, and slogan-like openings.
- Do not open with a binary contrast such as "This is not X" or "X is not Y" unless the contrast is the actual observed point.
- Prefer short active sentences. Cut repetition, filler, and abstraction.
- Start with the buyer's situation. Mention SpielOS only when it is relevant to that situation.
- Do not force a hook, proof point, CTA, link, product mention, or company reminder into every piece.
